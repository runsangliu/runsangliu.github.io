Sensor-based modeling,
monitoring, and control

ADDITIVE MANUFACTURING · QUALITY ENGINEERING

In situ imaging captures how an additive manufacturing process evolves, but its high dimensionality and dependence across layers make quality monitoring challenging. My work develops statistical methods to characterize this variation and identify abnormal process behavior.

In Situ Layerwise inspection and multiscale monitoring

I develop Gaussian process models for layerwise-dependent imaging data and a wavelet-based framework for multiscale analysis of variance and image-guided statistical process control.

Probabilistic modeling and characterization of melt pool morphology

I use photon-emission simulation and multimodal probabilistic modeling to characterize melt pool geometry and quantify the influence of process conditions on its variability.

Project support: NSF CMMI-2531899, NSF CMMI-2011289, and NIST Cooperative Agreement 70NANB19H127.

Self-organizing networks for
multiphysics modeling

COMPUTATIONAL MODELING · MANUFACTURING & HEALTHCARE

Network representations provide a compact way to describe the structure and interactions of complex physical systems. My research develops self-organizing networks to represent geometry and simulate spatial and temporal dynamics.

Thermal modeling in additive manufacturing

I develop network-based models of heat accumulation and transfer in geometrically complex parts, together with computational methods for model design and calibration.

Cardiac geometry and dynamics

My work spans network representation of heart geometry, simulation of cardiac electrical propagation, and modeling of mechanical contraction. These studies provide a connected framework for investigating cardiac multiphysics dynamics.

Project support: NIH R01 HL172292 and NSF CMMI-2531899.

Digital twinning
and model calibration

MODEL–DATA INTEGRATION · DYNAMIC SYSTEMS

Useful digital twins require methods that connect computational representations with observations. My work investigates the calibration of network-based models, from geometric structure to evolving physical dynamics.

Geometry calibration

I investigate how imaging data can guide the calibration of a self-organizing heart network, linking a compact representation with observed anatomy.

Calibration of electrical dynamics

I study computational methods for synchronizing cardiac electrical models with observations, extending network simulation toward digital twin operation.
