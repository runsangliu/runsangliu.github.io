ABOUT

Understanding systems.
Enabling better decisions.

I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Harold and Inge Marcus Department of Industrial and Manufacturing Engineering at Penn State, advised by Dr. Hui Yang. I am pursuing a dual-title degree in Industrial Engineering and Operations Research, with expected completion in May 2027.

My research combines physical, statistical, and data-driven modeling to improve the understanding of complex systems and support monitoring, prediction, and control. I develop methods for smart manufacturing and healthcare, with a focus on sensor-based process monitoring, machine learning, digital twins, and network science.

RESEARCH

Research overview →

Sensor-based modeling, monitoring, and control
